• Long meetings: recordings of several hours are now transcribed in parts, so a four-hour meeting gets a transcript and a note instead of stopping Distavo.
• A forgotten recording stops itself after 8 hours, is saved and transcribed, and Distavo tells you. Change the limit in Settings, Recording.
• If Distavo ever stops while processing a recording, that recording is set aside and you are told, instead of it being tried again at every launch.
• Safer notes: leaked working steps are removed, a summary that loops is retried and never lost, and every facts-ledger row is checked against the transcript.
• A recording that stops mid-conversation is flagged, and the summary says so instead of inventing next steps.
• Settings, Recording: an optional compact recording format (about half the size) and how much space your recordings use.
• Your own name now reaches the summary, so a misheard name or a colleague "standing in for" someone is not taken for you.
• Recordings dropped from a phone are dated from their file name or their own metadata.
• A warning when your microphone is silent while the call is audible, with the likely reason, and an optional switch to another microphone.
• An optional keyboard shortcut starts and stops a recording.
• Return now keeps the recording in the delete confirmation, and System Settings no longer opens by itself after Save.
