• Early preview, off by default: Distavo can now write your meeting notes entirely on this Mac using Apple Intelligence, with no summarisation server to install. Turn it on by setting "embedded_enabled" in Distavo's settings file, then choose "Built-in (this Mac)" as the summarisation backend. Long recordings are summarised in several passes, so notes are less detailed than a full Ollama model — Ollama remains the default.
• Summarisation now picks its engine through a single code path, so a recording still waits politely for your server to come back instead of being marked as failed.
