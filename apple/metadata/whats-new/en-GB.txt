• After you stop a recording, Distavo asks who was in the meeting — how many people, your role, who the others were — and uses that to name the speakers and write the notes and follow-up email from your side. Skip it any time, or turn it off in Settings.
• "Stop and delete recording": started recording by mistake? One menu item stops it and deletes it — nothing is saved or transcribed.
• "Process a recording with…": pick any recording, a model and a language, and get a second note beside the normal one, named after the model and language — the easy way to compare engines on a real meeting.
• Recordings under 15 seconds are set aside instead of failing; the menu offers to delete them. Adjust the minimum in Settings.
• "Benchmark this Mac" in Settings times each downloaded engine on 30 seconds of a recent recording and turns the model suggestion into a measurement.
• A new "Facts first" prompt (Settings → Summarisation) has the model identify the speakers with evidence and list every number, date, company and rate it heard before writing the notes — best with a capable Ollama model such as gemma4:26b. Your current prompt is unchanged unless you switch.
• Optional: shrink recordings to about a twentieth of their size once the note is written (Settings → Recordings; off unless you turn it on).
• Early warning when the meeting audio is silent 20 seconds into a recording, and the detected language now shows in the Activity menu and Settings.
