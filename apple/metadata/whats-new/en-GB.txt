• Language packs: switch on Hebrew, Thai, Welsh or Icelandic in Settings → Transcription, and meetings in that language are transcribed by a model trained for it — community fine-tunes of Whisper from ivrit.ai, Mahidol University, Bangor University and Reykjavík University, all running on your Mac. Off by default; nothing changes for languages you have not switched on.
• When the meeting-audio channel of a recording turns out to be silent, Distavo now downmixes the microphone channel to mono and evens out its loudness before transcribing, so a quiet remote party is no longer lost.
• The "Facts first" prompt keeps the inside/outside IR35 status wherever it appears and adds a set-up action when a contract requires your own limited company.
• On-device summaries (the optional Apple Intelligence path) now give each action its own deadline instead of stamping every row "post-engagement", list transcription corrections as "heard X, probably Y", and no longer repeat a paragraph across sections. The same rules sharpen the classic Ollama prompt.
• Built-in Whisper transcription now runs its decoder on the GPU: two to three times faster, and it no longer cuts a passage short when the Neural Engine is busy (seen on the Catalan models).
