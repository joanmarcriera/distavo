• Better notes for non-English meetings: the requested note language is stated more firmly, and for Catalan and Spanish Distavo checks the note and asks the model once more if it answered in another language.
• Fewer invented facts: each line of the facts ledger is checked against what was said. Lines the transcript does not support are removed, doubtful ones are marked, and the note says how many.
• Recovered dropped speech: stretches of a meeting that the built-in transcriber left out (this could happen with a custom word list) are transcribed again, and the note says how much was recovered.
• Fewer speaker mix-ups in built-in transcription: when the speaker label changes in the middle of a sentence, the rest of that sentence goes back to the person who was speaking.
• Speaker count from your participants: after a recording, the number of speakers follows the names you type, and you are warned if they do not match. Leave it on Auto and Distavo works it out.
• Automatic summary template: Distavo picks the template that fits the meeting, such as a client meeting or an interview, and says why at the end of the note. New "Consultancy / client meeting" template.
• Terms for this meeting: add names and jargon for one meeting when you stop recording.
• Optional "Catalan IT" correction pack for IT terms the Catalan models often mishear, such as ERP, firewall and backups.
• Learn from your corrections: after you edit a transcript, Distavo offers to turn your fixes into replacement rules for next time. Settings also shows when each vocabulary term was last heard.
• In-person meetings: tick "In-person meeting (microphone only)" and Distavo stops warning about missing computer audio.
• A warning when nothing is being recorded in the first 15 seconds, and a long silent stretch at the end of a Distavo recording is trimmed.
• Clearer language line at the end of the note, and the note owner is no longer shown as "Me (me)".
• Includes everything from 1.18: the Notes window, search inside notes, and regenerate in the queue.
