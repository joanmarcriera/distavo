• New Notes window (menu bar > Notes…): all your notes, newest meeting first. Select one and every action is there for that note: open, reveal, transcript, ask, export, regenerate, rename speakers, compare.
• An action that cannot run on a note is shown greyed out with the reason, for example "no saved transcript" or "no timestamps saved".
• The list updates by itself while it is open, and the note you selected stays selected.
• Search is now the field at the top of the Notes window: filter by title, or search inside notes and transcripts.
• Select several notes to export all their transcripts to a folder at once.
• Regenerate shows which note it will rewrite, and a regenerate waiting behind a recording appears in the Processing Queue, where it can be cancelled.
• The menu-bar menu is shorter: nine separate commands moved into the Notes window.
• After a recording, "Default from Settings" now says what the default is.
• Settings > Summaries: pick the bigger model from the models on your server, with a note on whether it really is bigger.
• Fixed: an extra instruction asking for an added line was sometimes left out of the note.
• Fixed: exported transcripts were named "Untitled" instead of after the note.
• Fixed: converting a recording could open an audio device and ask for the microphone.
