• Settings has a new sidebar layout with a Notes pane, a filter field and shorter explanations. Every existing setting is still there. Everything new below is off until you turn it on.
• Write notes in any language: English, the meeting's own language, or a language you choose. You can also set it per recording.
• Teach Distavo your names and jargon with a vocabulary list and find-and-replace rules.
• Summary templates for stand-ups, 1:1s, interviews, sales calls and lectures, plus your own; choose one per recordings subfolder or per recording.
• Regenerate a note with another template, model or instruction, without transcribing again. The previous version is kept.
• Optional Tasks and Decisions sections, a window listing open action items across your notes, and Send to Reminders.
• Search across all notes and transcripts, and Ask Your Notes, answered by your local model only.
• Open Transcript: play the recording, click a word to jump there, correct the text and re-summarise. Rename speakers across a note.
• Export a transcript as SRT, WebVTT, JSON, HTML, Word or PDF.
• While recording: Quick Notes that steer the summary, and key-moment markers with audio clip export.
• Optional prompt to record when a call app starts using the microphone, and calendar-aware titles and attendees.
• Shortcuts actions, a distavo:// link scheme and a Finder service; a Processing Queue window with drag and drop; Obsidian-friendly frontmatter, tags and vault copy.
