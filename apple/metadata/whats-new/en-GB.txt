• Watching the folder is now a saved setting: Settings, General, "Watch the folder for new recordings". "Pause watching" in the menu is the same switch, so a pause stays in place after a restart. Process now, Retry and Shortcuts still work while it is off.
• A pause chosen in the menu is no longer undone when the Settings window is saved or closed, and saving Settings while paused no longer starts processing.
• Where remembered voices are available: a Voices window (Settings, Transcription, Review Voices) lists every remembered voice with a clip from each recent meeting. Confirm a guess, say "Not this person", rename a voice or join two into one, with one step of undo. Voices not yet named are listed too. No audio is stored; clips are played from your own recordings.
