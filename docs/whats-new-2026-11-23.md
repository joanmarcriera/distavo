# What's new on feat/drive-1123-ideas (23 Nov 2026)

Built unattended by drive 2026-11-23-0030, lane distavo-build, from `distavo-ideas/BUILD-NOW.md`. No release, no
version bump. Every new setting is **off in your existing config** (your notes stay byte-identical) and on for a
fresh install, so you switch each one on yourself. Settings save to `~/Library/Application Support/Distavo/watcher-config.json`.

## 1. Models released after every file (memory growth, task 4019): partial
WhisperKit, SpeakerKit and the language detector now unload on every exit. This does **not** stop the growth: a new
soak test measured about 80 MB more per file before and after (+660 MB vs +706 MB over 9 files). It found where the
growth comes from: a fresh WhisperKit per file leaks about 50 MB even after unloading, while one reused instance stays
flat. No setting. Each file still reloads its model (about 4.4 s for the turbo model). Details and the next fix, which
needs your decision: `docs/memory-growth-2026-10-31.md`.
Try: nothing to switch on. Workaround as before: quit and reopen Distavo after a long recording day.

## 2. "Languages I speak" (D085)
Language detection picks only from your list, so a stray "Nynorsk 43%" on an English call is ignored and the
activity log says so once. The note footer still shows what the detector heard.
Try: Settings > Transcription > Spoken language "Automatic" > **Languages I speak**: add English, Catalan, Spanish.

## 3. Dates in tasks resolved against the recording date (D083)
"by Friday" in an action item becomes "by Friday (Fri 20 Nov 2026)". "next Monday" gets both possible dates with a
question mark. Lines that already hold a date are left alone. English, Catalan and Spanish.
Try: Settings > Notes > Checks on the note > **Resolve dates like "Friday" in tasks**, then Regenerate a note.

## 4. Warnings at the top of the note (D082)
A "Check this note" box under the title lists a cut-off recording, words removed by the offensive-word guard, one
person under several spellings (with "Check names in notes" on) and an unsure language detection. The footer keeps its lines.
Try: Settings > Notes > Checks on the note > **Warnings at the top of the note**.

## 5. Forgotten recordings stop by themselves (D027, tasks 6671, 6760)
While the silence lasts, the "no sound" notice comes back every few minutes. After 3 unanswered notices the recording
stops and is **kept**. The notice now offers Stop and keep, Keep recording, and Stop and discard (which asks first).
Try: Settings > Recording > "Suggest stopping after silence" on, then the stepper below it (0 = old behaviour). Check list:
`docs/meeting-capture-verification.md`, "Forgotten recordings".

## 6. Plan steps need a quote (D059)
"Highest-ROI follow-up", "30-minute post-meeting plan" and "Key people" keep only steps backed by a quote from the
meeting. A step whose quote is not found is marked [unverified]; a step with no quote is removed. Ollama summaries only.
Try: Settings > Notes > Checks on the note > **Plan steps need a quote from the meeting**, then Regenerate.

## 7. Split a recording into two meetings (D077, task 6668)
When back-to-back calls ended up in one recording, the Notes window offers the silence between them and cuts the
recording into two new ones, which become two notes. The original audio is untouched and the old note is kept as a backup version.
Try: Notes… > select the note > Change > **Split Recording…**, pick the silence, Split. Check list: `docs/notes-window.md`.
The halves are processed without a participants line (the "who was in this meeting?" question is not asked per half yet).

## 8. Day log (D102)
One file per day, `notes/day-logs/YYYY-MM-DD-day.md`: a timeline of the day's notes, the open action items, speakers
still unnamed and any warnings, each linked to its note. Day logs stay out of the Notes window and search.
Try: menu **Write Today's Day Log**, or `open "distavo://daylog?date=2026-11-23"` in Terminal.

Links may ask only for today or a day of the past year, at most once every 5 seconds, and never rewrite an existing
log. Writing it again from the menu keeps the previous version (edited or not) as a `.prev-` backup.
