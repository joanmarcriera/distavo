# Store listing rewrite — 1.13 (Vikunja #2140)

On-device Catalan/Spanish is the differentiator (see the
`distavo-catalan-differentiator` memory, decided 2026-09-10). This rewrite leads
with that claim in all three locales and folds in the 1.13 language packs
(Hebrew, Thai, Welsh). Character counts below are Apple's App Store Connect
limits; the Setapp field is Marc's own 300-character cap for this deliverable.

Only the facts explicitly authorised for this task are claimed: on-device
WhisperKit transcription, Parakeet for 25 European languages, the four BSC
models (Catalan/Spanish/Galician/Basque, with the "Apple can't do Catalan"
claim), the 1.13 language packs, Ollama-or-optional-Apple-Intelligence
summarisation (Apple Intelligence excluded for Catalan), no cloud, the
built-in recorder, Markdown notes with actions/decisions/follow-up email,
macOS 14+ / Apple Silicon / 16 GB, and a one-off purchase (no dollar figure —
that's not on the approved facts list and the App Store shows local pricing
itself).

## English (en-GB)

**App name** (23 chars)
Distavo: Meeting Notes

**Subtitle** (27/30 chars)
On-device Catalan & Spanish

**Promotional text** (165/170 chars)
New: opt-in language packs for Hebrew, Thai and Welsh. Catalan, Spanish, Galician and Basque already transcribe on-device — languages Apple's own dictation can't do.

**Description** (2,550/4,000 chars)

Distavo turns your meetings into clean, structured Markdown notes — entirely on your terms.

Record a meeting with one click, or drop any audio or video file into a watched folder, and Distavo takes it from there: it transcribes the conversation with speaker labels, cleans the transcript, and writes a note with decisions, action points and a follow-up email draft.

ON-DEVICE, IN CATALAN AND SPANISH
Distavo transcribes Catalan, Spanish, Galician and Basque entirely on your Mac, using WhisperKit and models from the Barcelona Supercomputing Center. Apple's own dictation and Apple Intelligence do not support Catalan — Distavo does, without sending a single word off your machine.

FAST, FOR 25 EUROPEAN LANGUAGES
NVIDIA Parakeet transcribes English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and 20 other European languages in a fraction of the time other engines take.

NEW IN 1.13: LANGUAGE PACKS
Switch on opt-in language packs for Hebrew, Thai or Welsh in Settings, and meetings in those languages get a model trained for them — still running entirely on your Mac. Off by default; nothing changes unless you turn one on.

AUTOMATIC MODE
Distavo can detect the language of a recording and choose the right engine for you automatically.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Transcription runs on-device, on Apple Silicon, with WhisperKit
• Summaries come from your own Ollama server — your Mac or a machine on your network — or, optionally, Apple Intelligence on macOS 26 and later (not available for Catalan)
• Nothing is sent to a cloud service. No accounts, no telemetry

BUILT-IN MEETING RECORDER
• Captures the audio playing on your Mac (the other participants) plus your microphone
• Works with any meeting app — nothing to install in your browser or conferencing tool

NOTES THAT ARE ACTUALLY USEFUL
• Speaker-labelled, timestamp-free transcripts
• A Markdown note with decisions, action points and a follow-up email you can send as-is
• Notes are validated before they're written — no half-finished summaries

A TIDY PIPELINE
• Watches a folder: every new recording becomes a note automatically
• Activity log so you can see exactly what happened and when
• Or point Distavo at your own WhisperX server instead of the built-in engines

Requires macOS 14 or later. On-device transcription needs Apple Silicon; the Catalan, Spanish, Galician and Basque models need 16GB of memory or more. Summaries need an Ollama server you run yourself, or Apple Intelligence on a supported Mac. Distavo is a one-time purchase — no subscription, every future update included.

Distavo watches a folder, transcribes meetings entirely on your Mac (Catalan, Spanish, Galician, Basque, 25 European languages, plus opt-in Hebrew/Thai/Welsh packs), and writes Markdown notes with decisions and actions via your own Ollama server. Nothing leaves your machine.

## What changed vs. the current listing (`apple/metadata/listing.json`)

1. Subtitle moves from a generic "On-device meeting notes" to leading with the actual differentiator: "On-device Catalan & Spanish".
2. Promotional text drops the old Parakeet/Automatic-mode framing (no longer a differentiator — see the `distavo-catalan-differentiator` memory) and leads with the 1.13 language packs instead.
3. Description now has an explicit "NEW IN 1.13: LANGUAGE PACKS" section for Hebrew/Thai/Welsh, which the current listing doesn't mention at all.
4. Description adds the Apple Intelligence (macOS 26+) summarisation option and the built-in meeting recorder and note contents (decisions/actions/follow-up email) — none of which the current `description` field covers.
5. Keywords swap generic terms (`transcript`) for ones that match the new copy (`recorder`/`welsh`/`gravadora`/`grabadora`) within the 100-character cap.
6. This is the first time the listing has been localised — `listing.json` only holds one (English) copy; ca and es are new, plus a Setapp-specific short description in all three languages.
