• Two more language packs (Settings → Transcription): Tamil, from IIT Madras's Speech Lab, and Norwegian — Bokmål and Nynorsk — from the National Library of Norway. Off by default, downloaded once when you switch them on. Both write without punctuation; the notes are unaffected.
• Settings now keeps Save in view at all times, shows "Unsaved changes" until you save, and saves pending changes when you close the window.
• A note that comes back cut short is no longer saved as finished: Distavo asks the summariser again, and only then reports a failure.
• The "Facts first" prompt no longer leaks its working steps ("Step 1…") into the note.
