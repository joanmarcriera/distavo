• People pages: one page per person you have met, built only from names you confirmed, with their meetings, role and organisation. Menu, Update People Pages.
• Meeting Brief: before a meeting, the last meetings and the open tasks for each person in it.
• Merge People joins two spellings of one person, and can be undone.
• Two meetings in one recording: split a recording in two from the Notes window, or let Distavo ask when a long recording with a quiet gap stops (Settings, Recording). Each meeting gets its own note and its own speakers.
• Transcript: Move to Speaker gives a selected part to another or a new speaker.
• Quick Notes: type the objective of the meeting and who is in it while you record.
• Day log: one note per day listing that day's meetings.
• Safer notes: every generated plan step needs a quote from the transcript, relative dates in tasks are resolved against the recording date, and warnings appear at the top of the note.
• "Languages I speak" limits language detection to your own short list.
• A names list keeps the spelling of the people and products you mention; tasks are only given to an owner the transcript supports.
• New templates for sales calls and for played-back courses, and a sales-call export.
• The silence notice repeats, then stops and keeps the recording; you can also stop and discard from the notice.
• Fixes: two recordings with the same cleaned-up name no longer collide, and an interrupted recording is never deleted in favour of a shorter copy.
